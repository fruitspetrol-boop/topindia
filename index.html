Indian Constitution: Protect Its Basic Principles

“A strong Constitution is the foundation of a strong democracy.”

It is fair to debate whether a particular constitutional amendment is necessary. But removing every amendment altogether would be a sweeping change, not a simple return to an untouched Constitution.

India's constitutional system allows amendments through Article 368, while the Supreme Court's basic-structure doctrine limits Parliament from destroying the Constitution's essential features.

Our view: preserve the Constitution's core principles, and assess each proposed amendment carefully, transparently and in the public interest.

🌟 Top Facts About India

🏛️

Capital

New Delhi is India's capital and an important centre of national government.

🗺️

Geography

India is the world's seventh-largest country by geographical area.

👥

Population

India is the world's most populous country, with more than 1.4 billion people.

🗳️

Democracy

India is a constitutional parliamentary republic with a large electorate.

💰

Currency

The Indian Rupee (₹), with the currency code INR.

🕰️

Time Zone

Indian Standard Time is UTC+05:30 throughout the year.

🏛️ Government & Constitution

  • Independence Day: 15 August 1947.
  • Republic Day: 26 January.
  • The Constitution came into effect on 26 January 1950.
  • India has 28 states and 8 Union Territories.
  • Parliament consists of the President, Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.
  • The Supreme Court is India's highest court.
  • The Election Commission of India administers elections under the Constitution and election laws.

🎭 Culture & Progress

🗣️

Languages

The Constitution recognises 22 scheduled languages. Hindi and English are used for Union government official purposes under applicable law.

🏛️

Heritage

India is known for historic monuments, architecture, literature, crafts and diverse cultural traditions.

🛰️

Space Science

ISRO has achieved major milestones, including the Chandrayaan missions and the Mars Orbiter Mission.

🌾

Agriculture

Agriculture supports millions of livelihoods and produces rice, wheat, cotton, tea, pulses and many other crops.

🏏

Sports

Cricket is hugely popular. India also has strong traditions in hockey, badminton, wrestling and athletics.

🏔️

Natural Beauty

From the Himalayas to deserts, forests, coastlines and islands, India's landscapes are remarkably varied.

🇮🇳 National Symbols

🇮🇳

National Flag

The Tiranga: saffron, white and green, with the navy-blue Ashoka Chakra.

🐅

National Animal

Bengal Tiger.

🦚

National Bird

Indian Peacock.

🪷

National Flower

Lotus.

🐬

National Aquatic Animal

Ganges River Dolphin.

🦁

National Emblem

The Lion Capital of Ashoka, with the motto “Satyameva Jayate” — Truth Alone Triumphs.

💡 Did You Know?

  • India's national anthem is “Jana Gana Mana”.
  • “Vande Mataram” is India's national song.
  • India's national calendar is based on the Saka Era.
  • India's diversity includes many cuisines, festivals, art forms and musical traditions.
